Chai Wala Tycoon

Privacy Policy • Effective Date: September 27, 2026

Privacy Summary: Chai Wala Tycoon is built to respect your privacy. The game does not collect personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or GPS location. Your game progress is saved locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Chai Wala Tycoon is a local single-player dhaba/tea stall management simulation game. We do not require you to create an account or sign in with personal credentials.

  • Local Gameplay Data: Game progression, coin balances, unlocked recipes, stove upgrades, and staff statuses are stored locally on your device using HTML5 Local Storage. This data remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Device & Crash Diagnostics: Standard, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as operating system version and crash logs) may be processed automatically through Android runtime systems to fix technical issues.

2. Advertising & Rewarded Videos

To keep the game free to play, Chai Wala Tycoon features optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g. watching an ad to earn bonus coins or temporary brewing speed boosts). These ads may be served by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob.

Ad networks may process non-sensitive device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID) in accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies to deliver relevant ads and prevent fraud.

3. Children's Privacy

Chai Wala Tycoon is designed for general audiences and all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete such records.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your game state resides exclusively on your local device. You can reset or delete your game data at any time from the in-game Settings menu by tapping "Start a new journey". Uninstalling the game or clearing application storage in Android Settings will permanently erase all local save data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page occasionally for any updates.
